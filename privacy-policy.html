Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 1, 2026

This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal information when you use our IELTS Practice Band 9 application ("the Service").

1. Information Collection and Account Information

We believe in privacy by design. You can use IELTS Practice Band 9 as a Guest without providing any personal identifying information (no name, email, or phone number required).

If you choose to link your account to enable Secure Cloud Backup and Cross-Device Synchronization, you may sign in using Sign in with Apple or Google Sign-In. In this case, we receive:

  • Account Identifiers: A unique User ID (UID) and, depending on your sign-in provider and privacy settings, your email address and display name.
  • Purpose of Collection: Strictly for Account Management and App Functionality — allowing you to securely authenticate, restore, and synchronize your personal IELTS learning progress across multiple devices.
  • No Third-Party Tracking: We do NOT sell, lease, or share your account credentials with third parties for cross-app advertising or tracking purposes.

2. Secure Cloud Backup & Cross-Device Synchronization

When you initiate a cloud backup, IELTS Practice Band 9 stores your learning progress securely using Google Cloud Firestore.

  • Data Synchronized: Your practice test history and scores (Reading, Listening, Writing, and Speaking), study streaks, vocabulary mastery status (learned and favorited words), writing essay drafts, and user preferences.
  • Access Control: All backup data is isolated per authenticated user account. Cloud database security rules enforce strict owner-only access: only your authenticated account can access, read, or write your personal data.
  • Device-Local Media: To protect your personal privacy and optimize data usage, voice audio recordings (.m4a/.wav) and raw local task images remain stored strictly on your local device storage. Only your transcribed answers, feedback metadata, and scores are synchronized to the cloud.

3. Account Deletion & Data Retention (User Rights)

We strictly respect your right to control and erase your data in compliance with Apple App Store and Google Play policies:

  • In-App Self-Service Deletion: You can permanently delete your account and all associated cloud data at any time directly within the application by navigating to Settings > Account > Delete Account and confirming your request.
  • Immediate & Permanent Purge: When you delete your account, your authentication record is removed immediately and all cloud backup records associated with your User ID on Google Cloud Firestore are permanently wiped. This action is irreversible.
  • Manual Deletion Requests: If you are unable to access the app, you may also request the deletion of your account and associated data by emailing us at ltsolutionviet@gmail.com with your registered email or User ID.

4. AI Evaluation & Generative AI Data Processing (Google Cloud AI)

IELTS Practice Band 9 provides AI-powered evaluation features to help users analyze their IELTS Writing essays and IELTS Speaking practice recordings. When you explicitly choose to submit your practice answers for AI scoring:

  • Data Transmitted: Only the specific user-provided practice answers (essay texts or recorded audio files) are sent to our AI evaluation pipeline powered by Google Cloud AI.
  • Purpose of Processing: Submissions are processed solely in real time for scoring IELTS criteria (Task Achievement/Response, Coherence & Cohesion, Lexical Resource, Grammatical Range & Accuracy, Pronunciation, and Fluency) and providing personalized constructive feedback.
  • Data Protection & No AI Training: Your submissions and audio recordings are not retained permanently on our servers. Neither IELTS Practice Band 9 nor Google Cloud AI uses your submitted texts or voice recordings to train, fine-tune, or improve any artificial intelligence models.
  • Explicit User Consent: Data is only transmitted after you give affirmative consent via our in-app disclosure prompt. You are free to cancel or decline AI evaluation at any time.

5. Log Data & Diagnostic Information

Whenever you use our Service, in the event of an application error or crash, we collect diagnostic information called Log Data through third-party services. This data may include device model, operating system version, app state at the time of crash, and anonymous performance statistics. This information is utilized solely to isolate and resolve software bugs.

6. Cookies

Cookies are not used within our mobile application.

7. Third-Party Service Providers

The app relies on trusted third-party service providers for infrastructure, analytics, and service delivery. You can review their respective privacy policies below:

  • Firebase Authentication
  • Google Cloud Firestore
  • Google Cloud AI
  • Google Analytics for Firebase
  • Firebase Crashlytics
  • Google AdMob

8. Advertising

This app may display advertisements via Google AdMob. AdMob may utilize standard mobile device identifiers (such as Android Advertising ID or iOS IDFA) subject to your operating system permissions to display relevant ads, cap ad frequency, and measure campaign performance.

9. In-App Purchases

All in-app purchases and VIP/Pro subscriptions are processed securely and directly by the Apple App Store (StoreKit) or the Google Play Store (Google Play Billing). We do not collect, process, or store your credit card numbers, payment details, or financial billing information.

10. Children’s Privacy

Our Services are not directed to children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. If we discover that a child under 13 has provided personal data, we take immediate steps to delete such information from our records.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically to reflect enhancements to our service or changes in regulatory standards. We encourage you to review this page periodically. Any updates become effective immediately upon posting to this URL.

12. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions regarding our Privacy Policy or data handling practices, please contact us at ltsolutionviet@gmail.com.
